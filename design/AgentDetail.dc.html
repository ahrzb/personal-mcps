Board notes
• Three panes. A rail of the apps claude holds a grant on (amber dot: an ask entry on that app; dash: dormant — the app is archived, or an entry matches nothing today; blue dot: an unsaved draft), then + Grant another app…, then the agent’s own panes: Credentials, Activity, Danger zone. The listing shows everything the app advertises — roles, tools, prompts, resources, and the agent’s pattern entries — and the details pane opens the selected row.
• One control on every row: none · ask · allow. Solid when set on that row itself; hollow when a role implies it, with the role named beneath (“via reader”). The states below what a role grants are disabled — lower the role to lower them. Highest wins: allow over ask. There is no deny; nothing granted is simply none (§7).
• Roles carry the same control. A role row lists its patterns per §20 family and how many items they match today; the built-in all is last. A tunneled app declares its roles at connect; a proxied app’s come from config.
• Direct entries beside roles. Setting a tool, prompt or resource on its own row writes an inline entry tool/<name>, prompt/<name> or resource/<uri> into the same grant set (role names never contain /, so the two never collide). Text typed into the filter that is not one exact name is offered as a pattern; pattern entries live in their own group with what they match today.
• One draft per app. A changed row carries the amber dashed unsaved badge, the rail entry the blue dot, the foot counts the changes with Discard / Save — Save is one grant_set for the (agent × app) pair, replacing the set. Switching app or pane with a draft shows the save-or-discard banner (on AgentDetailStates).
• Remove from claude sits in the foot: grant_set with an empty set. History stays; a waiting request expires.
• Every column reads from ops that exist: agent_list and app_get (catalog, roles), token_list, connection_list, approval_list, audit_query. Header tiles are the agent’s totals across its apps.
