Board notes
• One column, and the window set without a drag. Touch has no hover and no precise drag, so the brush is not drawn at narrow: the 1h · 24h · 7d presets and tapping a day on the axis set the window, and the tapped day is marked. The lanes stay — each name sits above its cells, and the cells lose their 1 px gaps so an hour is still a readable band at this width.
• The rail becomes Filters · N, a full-screen level headed ‘ Audit with the same groups at 44 px rows, the same exhaustive counts and Show all N, and a sticky Show N events at the foot. The level names itself, so the rail’s own “Filter” title is dropped there — a listing header never repeats what the level header shows.
• The record is a level, not a drawer, headed ‘ Audit; Close belongs to the pointer rendering. Every id in the field table keeps its 44 px target, which is why the table reads as a list of rows here rather than the two tight columns the drawer draws.
• An event card is two lines. The argsHead preview is the one thing the phone drops — it is the widest and the least answerable at this width, and the record is one tap away. 768–1023 keeps the desktop layout with the rail above the main pane as a wrapping row of groups.
• Same shell as the other phone boards (MobileAppDetail, MobileAgentDetail): the brand and a hamburger whose sidebar carries the five nav entries, the Approvals count and Sign out.
