Board notes
• A new pane, Recording, in the App group between Roles and Overview; its rail marker is a dot lit when body logging is on (the Two-factor marker, reused). Overview loses its three recording rows to it and keeps identity only.
• One form, one app_update: log_bodies, redact, redact_results — the three fields the op already takes for either kind. The pane lands back on itself with the notice, like every other pane mutation.
• The switch is a checkbox; with scripting on it enables the Save button and, on a proxied app, opens the warning and focuses the first empty redaction row. With scripting off everything is already visible and Save works as drawn.
• Redaction rows are plain inputs, one row per tool-or-pattern, paths comma-separated — the YAML’s shape, flattened. The route composes the two objects the way the grant editor composes roles.
• Schema-declared masking is stated, not edited: it comes from the app’s own schemas. A schema-unsound tool would be named here with the §13 sentence.
