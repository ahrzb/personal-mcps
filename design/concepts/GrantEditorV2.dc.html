Board notes
• Same op, same rows. Still one page per (agent × app), one three-way choice per role, Save composing grant_set’s roles list — the shipped editor’s contract. What is new is what each row says about itself and the reach card under the rows.
• A role row shows its patterns per family and what they match today, counted with the door’s own matcher over the same catalog the app page reads. A bare list is spelled out as tools-only, with the other families shown as “grants nothing here” when the app advertises that family — the case the agent page flags.
• The reach card is the agent page’s Access block for one app, recomputed on each change with script; with scripting off it shows the saved set, and the agent page shows the new one after Save. Overlap is resolved the way the gate does: allow wins over approval per tool, and the chip names the role that won.
• Roles themselves are not edited here. A tunneled app declares them at connect; a proxied app’s are owner-defined. The Roles pane on the app page owns that editor.
• Entry points, all to this page: the Agents list’s Grant… select, the agent page’s Edit grants and “Grant access to another app…”, and the app page’s Agents pane. One editor, four doors.
