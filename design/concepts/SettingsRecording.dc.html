Where bodies show
The expanded row on /audit, the approval detail, and the JSONL export — all post-redaction.
A switch here is the app’s own setting — the same one on /apps/<slug>/recording, where its redaction paths are edited. Turning a proxied app on without masked paths records whatever its tools take, in the clear, for the retention window.
Board notes
• Settings is the owner’s view of what holds a way in; this pane is the owner’s view of what is kept about them. A third rail group, Records, keeps it out of Sign-in and Access. Marker: apps on of apps total, the builtin excluded.
• Each switch is one app_update { log_bodies } posted from this pane and landing back on it — a per-row form, like Revoke on Tokens. No bulk action: there is no bulk op, and five apps do not need one.
• The row’s counts are audit_query { app } totals; “with bodies” is the rows carrying the body columns. Masked paths are app_get’s redact maps counted, plus the tunneled schema’s writeOnly tools.
• The Limits card is read-only: retention and the cap are env settings, and pretending otherwise would draw a control that does nothing.
• The builtin row has a fixed switch rather than none, so the column never reads as “unknown” for the one app whose bodies are always kept.
